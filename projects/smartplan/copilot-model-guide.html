This page is meant as a quick reference on the Copilot CLI roster and what each model is good at. It's distilled from smartplan's model registry. If you only want a pick, start at Quick picks by task.

How to read the numbers:

  • Prices are $/1M tokens in/out from GitHub's pricing table, mostly read 2026-08-22 to 2026-10-13. The roster was confirmed live on 2026-09-02.
  • Terminal-Bench means version 4.0 unless a figure names 2.1 or 2.0. A rank like (#7) is from the official tbench.ai board.
  • vals.ai archived its SWE-bench Verified board on 2026-09-21. Its figures are frozen, so a model released after that never gets one.

Terms used below. A verifier checks another model's work for correctness. A cross-family verifier comes from a different vendor than the model it checks. That helps it catch mistakes a same-family reviewer might share. ctx is the context window. A surcharge is a higher per-token rate once a request passes a stated context size. Cache read, cache write and cache-hit prices are discounted rates for reusing text from an earlier request.

Reading the Tiers

  • Reasoning models plan, verify work from the Mid and Strong tiers, and take on irreducibly cross-cutting tasks. They never do first-pass implementation or mechanical work, which would waste their price.
  • Implementer (Mid) models are the floor for briefed, correctness-sensitive execution. They also verify Cheap-tier output.
  • Mechanical (Cheap) models handle single-concern tasks, always behind a verification gate. A model priced below Haiku stays a trial candidate until it earns a seat. GPT-5.6 Luna holds the Copilot coding-leaf floor on board evidence and the author's call, with no trial yet. GPT-6 Luna is listed ahead of it, also untrialed. We record outcomes before any other floor moves.

Roster Footnotes

  • Vendor numbers vs. official boards: compare within one source class. Official boards run standardized, often minimal, harnesses and score far lower than vendor runs. swebench.com's Verified board tops out at 79.2 and Scale's SWE-bench Pro public board at 61.5. Unlabeled SWE-bench figures are vendor or system-card numbers. vals.ai doesn't always order models the way the official Terminal-Bench board does.
  • Grok: grok-4.6 went GA on 2026-08-14, plain Pro included. grok-4.5 left Copilot on 2026-10-19, with Grok 4.7 its successor. Claude Code can't offer an xAI model. Copilot CLI's BYOK path can point at any OpenAI-compatible endpoint, on your own key and bill.
  • Auto routes each request by task complexity. Since 2026-10-28 it shows as Copilot Fusion in the CLI, VS Code, the app and JetBrains, at 30% off model costs on paid plans through 2026-11-30 (Fusion Standard on every plan, High and Max on Pro+ and up). The 10% Auto discount now covers Chat and cloud agent only. It fits inline or fast-path sessions. In a tiered dispatch wave, pin the model explicitly. (model: pins are reported as downgraded when the session runs a 0x-cost-tier model.)
