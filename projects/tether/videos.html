Troubleshooting

When A Ref Breaks

A ref can stop resolving for three reasons: its target is gone, its target lost its Tether component, or its target was copied and got a new ID. Each video shows what the editor tells you, and the fix.

The Reference page is the full technical reference, and the wiring diagram shows how the pieces connect.
