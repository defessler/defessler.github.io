Quick reference

Every table on the site, condensed into one page: DokiDex's own commands and model tiers, the model-name decoder, quantization and VRAM math, and the current closed- and open-weight landscape, for when you already understand the concept and just need the number.

This page introduces nothing new. Every figure below is lifted from a page elsewhere on this site, where the reasoning, the sourcing, and the caveats live in full. Come here when you already know what a term means and just need the exact flag, the exact number, or the exact row.

Only one large tier is ever resident on the GPU at once: llama-swap evicts and swaps on request. The one exception is the agent-seat group, which pins the Qwen3.8-27B [256k] (Candidate, Standard) seat and its 3 GB sidecar together. The local-CLI skills (doki-max, doki-think, doki-work, doki-cheap) each name one of the four everyday models directly, no alias in between: gpt-oss-120b [128k] (Reasoning, Heavy), Qwen3.8-27B [256k] (Candidate, Standard), Qwen3-Coder-30B-A3B [128k] (Coding, Light), and Qwen2.5-3B-Instruct [32k] (Utility, Free). Qwen3-Coder-30B-A3B [128k] (Coding, Light) won the daily-driver seat over higher-scoring candidates because promotion requires both ≥91% on the golden suite and zero tool-call flakes on the native gate. A tied-on-suite candidate (Qwen3.6-35B-A3B) hangs the native gate and stays off the agentic path.

VRAM estimation

Tip: VRAM (GB) ≈ Total_Parameters(B) × Bytes_per_Weight × 1.05–1.20 + KV-cache overhead

KV cache is a separate, context-length-dependent term:

GQA:  kv_bytes_per_token = 2 × n_layers × n_kv_heads × head_dim × bytes_per_element
MLA:  kv_bytes_per_token = n_layers × (kv_lora_rank + qk_rope_head_dim) × bytes_per_element

Key idea: for MoE, capacity sizing uses total parameters (every expert must be resident). Speed sizing uses active parameters only. A 671B/37B-active model needs VRAM for 671B and generates at roughly the speed of a 37B model.

MLA’s KV cache is dramatically smaller than GQA’s at the same context: roughly 68.6 KB/token for DeepSeek-V3 versus 192-328 KB/token for GQA models of comparable class, a 2.7-4.7× reduction. DokiDex’s own agent sessions measure 92-99% of each turn’s prefill served from prefix cache, which is why a local agent stack feels responsive despite resending its whole context every turn.
