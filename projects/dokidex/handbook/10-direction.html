:: THE COMPASS

COMPASS

Direction

The forward options, framed for a decision. What each avenue costs, what it buys, and what kind of stack it makes DokiDex become.

The chapters before this one described what exists. This chapter is the decision surface: the credible avenues forward, each with its evidence base, rough cost, and the part inventories usually skip, which is what kind of stack choosing it makes this become. These are competing bets, not a backlog. The framing question for each is “is this the identity?”

The avenues

1 · Token economy (CLI wave 5), make the local models punch above their weight

Chosen and in flight as of 2026-07-12. The arc’s planning pass produced its own load-bearing finding: on one GPU with llama-swap, the dominant serving win is 92 to 99% prompt-cache reuse, so cloud-derived cheap-tier patterns regress if copied naively. Routing work to a different tier evicts the resident model and its cache. Every leg was reshaped around that.

Landed: skill governance (disable-model-invocation + skillOverrides, which make heavy skills cost zero context until run) · cache-safe context compression (folded into the compaction path, which already re-prefills) · opt-in housekeeping-tier plumbing (default is the session model, with the tradeoff documented in-code) · the architect+editor mode (/architect: plan turns → gpt-oss-120b [128k] (Reasoning, Heavy), act turns → Qwen3-Coder-30B-A3B [128k] (Coding, Light), bound to the plan/act boundary so it costs one swap per plan cycle, and default-off pending its eval, docs/scorecards/2026-07-12-architect-editor-eval.md). In flight: LSP-backed context (clangd as a read-only tool, worth more than any grep for UE-sized C++, and absence-first since clangd isn’t assumed installed). Deferred behind LSP: the PageRank repo map (LSP may subsume much of its value for C++/C#, changing the calculus).

Still true: this is the arc where local-model economics (zero marginal tokens, one user) genuinely diverge from cloud designs. Makes it: a sharper daily tool.

2 · The UE lane, specialize where the owner actually lives

The ingredients exist and have already produced results: 8 UE golden tasks, the ue-delegates skill whose glob-triggered injection took delegate wiring from failing to 3/3, and a dedicated research doc on UE-capable local models. Deepening means: more engine-domain skills (UPROPERTY lifetimes, replication patterns, Build.cs hygiene), a UE eval expansion, and evaluating a fine-tuned/specialized coder tier for engine C++.

Costs: moderate, mostly authored knowledge plus eval time, not architecture. Buys: outsized quality exactly where the stack gets used most, because the passive-catalog A/B proved authored, triggered knowledge moves small models more than anything else measured here. Makes it: a gameplay programmer’s instrument rather than a general tool. Compounds with avenue 1. The two together are the “daily weapon” strategy.

3 · Model pruning to purpose, a research bet

REAP-style expert pruning scoped to the actual workload: keep the C++/C#/PowerShell experts, drop the Ruby. A pruned 30B that behaves like its full self on this repo’s tasks would buy context headroom and speed simultaneously. The evidence so far is cautionary. The one REAP candidate evaluated scored 7/15 (47%) with three hard timeouts and was parked (docs/scorecards/2026-07-06-claw-Qwen3-Coder-REAP-25B-A3B [128k] (Candidate, Light).md), and pruning research moves fast.

Costs: GPU-days and eval cycles with a real chance of “measured worse, rejected.” Buys: potentially a persistent hardware-multiplier, plus genuine research findings. Makes it: a research rig. Right move only after avenues 1 and 2 plateau, or as a background curiosity.

4 · Desktop & product polish, finish the migration and ship the artifact (closed, 2026-09-02)

This avenue used to read: finish the Electron migration (retire the WPF-vs-Electron split and the three version counters), settings panel, inline diff view, context meter, live preview in the Code workspace, and the C1 through C7 identity taste calls waiting on owner input, plus the media web-app direction if the media half was the draw. The owner didn’t take it. The desktop app, the Electron shell, and the whole media-generation half of the stack were removed on 2026-09-02 rather than finished (archive.md names the commit), which closes the “two shells, three versions” honesty debt by deleting one side of it instead of converging on the other. Nothing under this avenue is a live option any more.

5 · Parity endgame, declare victory deliberately (moot, 2026-09-02)

This avenue used to ask whether to track or freeze the generated parity ledger that scored DokiDex’s own agent against Claude Code, after Round 4 closed the last two gaps to a brief 99.1% and a 2026-07-12 probe-honesty re-audit rescored two over-credited items downward to a final 97.6%. The question is moot now. The agent and the ledger both went with the 2026-09-02 removal. Measuring your own clone against an upstream stops being a useful instrument once you ship the upstream, which is the same conclusion chapter 7 reaches.

Cross-cutting debts (do these regardless)

Small, known, and they compound if ignored: the voice-extension 404 bug (config points at .wav files the TTS route can’t serve) · the stale tests/doki-notts.test.ps1 · the InstallRoot-points-at-D:\Programs dev-run gotcha (DOKIDEX_HOME needs setting for studio runs from the repo) · the documentation staleness this handbook round is already retiring.

How to choose

Strip the options to identities and the choice gets easier:

If DokiDex is… Then invest in…
your daily weapon (most likely, given usage) 1 + 2: token economy, then the UE lane on top
a product for others 4 is closed. The desktop shell was removed rather than finished, so a new product surface would be a fresh avenue, not a resumption
a research rig 3: pruning + the model lane, eval-gated as always
a finished artifact 5 is moot. This handbook is the closest thing to a capstone now that the ledger it tracked is gone

The avenues aren’t exclusive forever. The next arc should still be one of them, chosen on purpose. The stack’s own history argues for how to decide: pick the bet, define the gate that would prove it wrong, and let the ledger keep the score.

End of the handbook. The source of truth is the tree. When they disagree, the tree wins and this document gets a fix.
