The chapter numbers have gaps, and they are deliberate. Chapters 2 (the agent loop), 5 (protocols) and 6 (the desktop app) were removed on 2026-09-02 along with the code they documented: DokiDex’s own C++ agent and its Electron app. The surviving chapters kept their numbers so every published URL still resolves. archive.md in the repo names the commit.

This is the engineering handbook for DokiDex, a fully-local agentic coding stack running on one RTX 5090, Windows 11 native, no cloud AI at runtime. It exists to answer two questions properly:

  1. How does this actually work? Not at the press-release level. At the level a senior C++ gameplay programmer expects: the memory budget, the serialization format, the authority model, the failure modes. Chapters 1, 3 and 4, with Unreal-native analogies where they’re precise and dropped where they’d be decoration. Quantization really is block compression.
  2. How do we know any of that is true, and where do the ideas come from? Chapters 7 and 8: the evals and proof machinery that keeps a claim attached to a measurement, then a curated study path for the theory underneath.
  3. What can it do, and what should it become next? Chapters 9 and 10: the full capability inventory, then the strategic options laid out for a decision, not a wishlist.

Everything here is written against the real tree (paths and line references point into the repo) and the measured record (docs/decisions.md, evals/results.jsonl). Where a claim is a number, it was measured. Where a choice was a judgment call, the why is stated inline. Stale statements are bugs. File them like bugs.

Start at The Big Picture if you’re new. If you already know what a context window is and just want the serving layer, start at Serving Local Models and read forward. The References chapter is a curated study path if you want the theory under the practice: papers, docs, and videos worth their runtime.

  1. :: THE FOUNDRY The Big Picture One machine, one GPU, one resident model. What runs where, and the budget that shapes all of it.
  2. :: THE FORGE Serving Local Models llama.cpp, GGUF, quantization as block compression, the MoE trick that fits 120B in 32 GB, and the roster that staffs the stack.

  5. :: THE CODEX References & Study Path The papers, docs, and videos under this handbook, ordered as a study path, each with a reason to spend the time.
  6. :: THE ARSENAL Capabilities The full inventory. Everything the stack does today, by surface, with the honest caveats attached.
  7. :: THE COMPASS Direction The forward options, framed for a decision. What each avenue costs, what it buys, and what kind of stack it makes DokiDex become.
