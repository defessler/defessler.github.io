Privacy

Covers dougfessler.com and the project sites under it, like /DokiDex/ and /Mediarr-Installer/. Last updated October 2, 2026.

What's collected

These sites use Google Analytics 4 to see which pages get read and what gets clicked. It records:

  • pages viewed, and the site that sent you here, if any
  • approximate location: country, region and city. Google estimates this from your IP address and says Google Analytics doesn't log or store IP addresses.
  • browser, operating system, device type and screen size
  • how far you scroll, clicks on links that leave the site, and file downloads
  • which links and buttons you click: their label and where they point, with email addresses removed
  • which sections of a page stay on screen for at least a second (by heading)

Google Analytics sets two first-party cookies, _ga and _ga_YQB4BCN1CH, so a returning browser counts as the same visitor.

What isn't

  • keystrokes, or anything you type into a page
  • form contents
  • mouse movement, and no session recordings
  • your IP address. I never see it.

Analytics isn't linked to any advertising account, Google signals is off, and nothing is sold or shared.

How long it's kept

Event-level data is deleted after 2 months and visitor-level data after 14 months. Totals in the standard reports, like page views per day, are kept longer.

Opting out

  • If your browser sends Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track, the click and section tracking doesn't run. Page views are still counted.
  • To block Google Analytics completely, use a content blocker or Google's opt-out browser add-on.
